About Me

A marketer who knows
India from the inside

I started my career at Lowe Lintas — one of India's most respected advertising agencies — where I learned how great campaigns are built from a deep understanding of people, not just demographics. That foundation of cultural intelligence and storytelling has shaped everything since.

Over the next decade, I worked across agencies and brands — building 360° campaigns for Taneira by Titan, Amazon India, and TI Cycles — before moving into product marketing at Zoho Corporation, where I've been driving subscription growth and product adoption across the India market.

At Zoho, I led app marketing for Zoho Show and WorkDrive (including ASO strategy), ran hyper-localised campaigns across metros and Tier-2 cities, and marketed AI-powered product features to real users. I'm also an early practitioner of AEO and GEO — optimising content for AI-powered search, a discipline almost no marketer in India is actively working on yet.

What makes me different: I don't just execute campaigns. I represent India's voice internally — pushing for locally relevant messaging, regional creative, and culturally grounded strategy that respects how Indian consumers actually think.

India Expertise

I don't just know India.
I market in it.

Understanding India as a market means understanding many Indias. A campaign that works in South Mumbai will fail in Patna. A message that lands in English won't resonate in Tamil. I've been navigating this complexity for 10 years — building campaigns that work across the full spectrum, from premium metro audiences to Bharat consumers discovering a brand for the first time.

01
Bharat vs Metro — Real Campaigns, Not Theory
I've actually run separate creative and messaging for Bharat and metro audiences. For TI Cycles, this meant distinct regional campaigns. For Zoho, it meant hyper-localised digital creative for different states. The insight always starts with the user, not the brief.
02
Cultural Depth in Campaigns
Building Taneira's brand identity taught me how to use cultural truth as a campaign foundation — not a surface-level reference. From festive timing to regional identity to the role of craft in modern Indian life.
03
Agency Network Across Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore
Ten years working across agencies and brands means I know the creative studios, media buyers, production houses, and PR agencies that deliver. I know who to trust, who works fast, and who understands specific audience segments.
04
India's Real Media Landscape
I understand where Indian users actually spend time — OTT, YouTube, Instagram Reels, WhatsApp, regional language platforms — and how to reach them cost-effectively. Not just the obvious channels, but the ones that actually work by geography and demographic.
05
App Behaviour in the Indian Ecosystem
Ran ASO for Zoho Show and WorkDrive — learning how Indian users discover and download apps differently from Western markets. Price sensitivity, regional language behaviour, and seasonal download patterns are all distinct here.
06
Being India's Voice Internally
At Zoho, I've pushed back on global templates that don't work for Indian users and championed market-specific messaging, localised creative, and India-appropriate campaign calendars. I know how to advocate for the local market within a larger organisation — diplomatically, but firmly.
